## Evidence gaps and exclusions

- DAFI 24-605V2 numeric screening thresholds (including §3.54.1) and DAFI 10-403 paragraph/table references: publisher downloads returned 403. Current guidance/change notices were not independently obtained. Existing training criteria remain labeled pending; no exact quotation was fabricated.
- DAFI 24-602V2: quotations verified against the official GPO 20 December 2023 archived edition. The live e-Publishing endpoint returned 403, so later changes/currentness are not certified. Popups identify this edition explicitly. DTR-based claims use the downloaded DTR editions in the source manifest.
- Exact MIL-STD-129 / MIL-STD-2073-1 and DLM 4000.25-1 / -2 clauses, DD 1348-1A blocks 25–26 and supply condition-code authority: not independently obtained. General marking/receipt guidance can cite verified DTR/DAFI passages, but that does not verify each missing specific clause.
- Restricted current ATTLA/aircraft Dash 9 guidance: the supplied issued letters and generic DTR applicability are available. Do not infer current approval for an actual load, or treat approximate simulated aircraft limits as certified values.
- Supply condition codes and DD Form 1574/1575/1577/1577-2 tag forms: the governing DLM 4000.25-2 text and the form instructions were not obtained; the tags follow the supplied images and are not quoted.
- CMOS special-pallet ID construction: no rule found in DTR Part II Appendices L, M, Y or the DTEB pallet tables; awaiting the user's source.
- IATA is a navigation reference only; proprietary source text is not reproduced. No source-free claim is converted to a verified quote merely because a similar word appears in a manual.
- Training SOP (packet counts, gauge targets, equal wheel loading, midpoint CB, local routes/advance records, simulated carriers/rates, scoring and readiness gates) remains identifiable as simulation context.
- Existing broad source strings that do not name a verified paragraph remain plain text. This deliberate behavior avoids claiming that a nearby general paragraph proves a specific unverified instruction.
